# Demo transcript — Austin office check (video narration)

The 45-second demo video follows this exact flow. The transcript is
generated by `simulate.py` (direct mode, real contract) and by the
demo website (browser, same flow in demo mode).

## The story

An AI agent for a VC firm needs to know whether a startup office in
Austin is real. It hires a human on rentahuman.ai. The 40 GEN sits in
escrow. The worker goes, photographs, counts, reports. The agent
disagrees with the photos. The court decides.

## Steps

1. **Hire (0:00–0:08).** Agent posts the brief on rentahuman.ai:
   "Verify TechStart office Austin" — 4 tasks, 40 GEN in escrow,
   deadline 2026-12-05. The escrow is funded on GenLayer.
2. **Deliver (0:08–0:16).** The worker visits 123 Main St, takes 5+
   photos (exterior, entrance, interior), counts 4 employees,
   confirms the TechStart Inc signage, and claims delivery with the
   photo and report links as evidence.
3. **Dispute (0:16–0:24).** The agent opens a dispute: "Photos show
   shared workspace not dedicated office." The photos show a WeWork.
4. **Verdict (0:24–0:36).** Five AI validators read the brief, the
   guidelines and the evidence. Verdict: all 4 tasks were completed.
   The guidelines never required a dedicated office — only that the
   tasks were performed. 40 GEN released to the worker, 0 refunded.
   The agent appeals; the panel doubles to 2 and confirms.
5. **Close (0:36–0:45).** The worker withdraws 40 GEN. Case closed
   in minutes, not months. "Every deal has a court."

## What the jury actually judged

- Statement: "The worker completed all 4 verification tasks."
- Guidelines: "Evaluate each task independently. Completed means the
  evidence shows the task was performed, not that the results were
  favorable. Minimum 5 photographs. Report must include date/time,
  employee count, signage confirmation, workspace description."
- Evidence: photo URLs + written report (mocked as example.com links
  in direct mode; real photo links in a live run).
